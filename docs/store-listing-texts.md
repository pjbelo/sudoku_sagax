**Full description**
Fill the grid, sharpen your mind.

Sudoku Sagax is classic sudoku with a clean, modern design. Place the digits 1 to 9 so that every row, column and 3×3 box contains each one exactly once. Every puzzle is freshly generated, has a single solution and can be solved by logic alone — no guessing needed.

NINE LEVELS, BEGINNER TO EXPERT
Start with a gentle Beginner grid and work your way up to Expert. Easier levels can be solved one deduction at a time; at Hard and Expert you will need pencil notes and sharper techniques. There is always a challenge that fits.

FEATURES
• 9 difficulty levels, from 46 to 24 given numbers
• A new puzzle every game, always with a unique solution
• Notes: pencil in the candidates of a cell
• Undo and erase
• Timer with pause — or switch it off
• Show errors — optional, off by default
• Hint button fills in a correct number (+30 s) — optional
• Scoreboard with your 10 best times per level
• Relaxing music and sound effects (can be turned off)
• Clean, distraction-free interface, made for phones and tablets
• No ads, no in-app purchases
• Play offline, anywhere

GOOD FOR YOUR BRAIN
Sudoku is pure deduction: no arithmetic, just logic. Large studies link regular number-puzzle play with better attention, reasoning and memory, and long-term research associates mentally stimulating games with healthier cognitive ageing. The in-app Brain Benefits section summarises the science, with full scientific references.

Whether you have two minutes or twenty, Sudoku Sagax is a calm, satisfying way to keep your mind sharp.
