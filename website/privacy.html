Privacy Policy

Last updated: 8 October 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how Tekinsight — Information Technologies ("we", "our" or "Tekinsight") collects, uses and protects information from users of the Sudoku Sagax application ("App" or "Sudoku Sagax"), published under the Sagax Games brand.

Summary: Sudoku Sagax does not collect personally identifiable data. We only use Google Firebase services to collect anonymous usage and stability data in order to improve the App. Your options and scores stay on your device.

1. Information We Collect

The App does not collect, store or process any personally identifiable data, such as name, email address, phone number or precise location.

Through Firebase, we automatically collect anonymous, aggregated data, namely:

  • Device type and operating system;
  • Device language;
  • App usage events (e.g. levels selected, games started, hints used, levels completed, options changed);
  • Approximate session duration;
  • Crash reports and error diagnostics (Firebase Crashlytics);
  • Country/region (based on IP address, without storing the IP).

2. How We Use the Information

The anonymous data collected is used exclusively to:

  • Understand how users interact with the App;
  • Identify and fix crashes and technical problems;
  • Improve the user experience and the App's performance;
  • Make informed decisions about future features and updates.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent or share any user data with third parties, except as described in this policy. The anonymous data collected by Firebase is processed by Google in accordance with the Google Privacy Policy.

4. Third-Party Services

The App uses the following third-party services:

  • Firebase Analytics (Google) — anonymous usage analytics;
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Google) — crash reporting. Learn more.

These services may collect information used to identify the user's device (such as installation or device identifiers), as described in their respective policies.

5. Children's Data

The App is not specifically directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal data from children. If we become aware that personal data from a child has been collected, we will take immediate steps to delete it.

6. Data Storage

The App plays fully offline. Your options, chosen language and scoreboard are stored only on your device and are never sent to us; they are deleted when you uninstall the App. Analytics and crash data are processed and stored on Google Firebase servers, in accordance with their security and retention policies.

7. Security

We employ reasonable measures to protect the information collected. However, no method of transmission over the Internet or electronic storage is 100% secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

8. Your Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and other applicable legislation, you have the right to:

  • Request information about the data collected;
  • Request the deletion of any data associated with your device;
  • Opt out of analytics data collection through your device settings;
  • Contact us to exercise any of your rights.

9. Changes to This Policy

We reserve the right to update this Privacy Policy at any time. Any changes will be published on this page with the revised update date. We recommend reviewing this page periodically.

10. Contact

For questions, requests or clarifications regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at sagax-games@tek-insight.com.
